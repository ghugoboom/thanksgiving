The House Rules

  1. I.

    Watch Your Kids

    The floors are uneven and everything nice in this house is breakable. Your kids are your job today.

  2. II.

    No Arguing

    Blessed are the peacemakers.

  3. III.

    Respect and Love

    Treat everyone with respect and love. Don’t be the one who ruins Thanksgiving by being a twat.

  4. IV.

    Know Your Limit

    You are not fun when you’re drunk. Don’t make a fool of yourself.

  5. V.

    You Break It, You Bought It

    Spill it, stain it or break it, and you clean it, fix it or replace it.

  6. VI.

    English in Company

    Speak English around other people. Otherwise we will assume you’re talking about us. It’s rude.

  7. VII.

    Not Your Party

    Don’t try to run the day. If you want to call the shots, host at your house and foot the bill.
